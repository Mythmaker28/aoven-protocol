This is a deeply personal question, and I won't pretend there's a clean answer I can hand you. But here are some things worth sitting with:

**Forgiveness and reconnection are two different decisions.**
You can forgive someone — release the grip the resentment has on you — without inviting them back into your life. You can also reconnect cautiously without having "forgiven" in any final sense. Conflating the two often pressures people into contact they aren't ready for.

**Some questions that might clarify things for you:**

- *What is he asking for?* An apology delivered? A relationship rebuilt? Practical help? Closure before something (illness, age)? The ask shapes what "yes" would even mean.
- *What would you want from a best-case version of this?* If you can't picture anything you'd actually want from him, that's information.
- *What's the cost of a low-stakes first step?* A letter or a single conversation is not a lifelong commitment. You can gather data before deciding anything bigger.
- *Who benefits from your forgiveness?* If it's primarily him — to ease his guilt — that's a different calculation than if it would free something in you.
- *What does the 20-year-old wound actually need?* Sometimes it's an apology. Sometimes it's an explanation. Sometimes it's just to be witnessed. Sometimes none of those will land, and the work is internal regardless of what he does.

**A few cautions:**

- Beware of timelines that aren't yours. "He's reaching out now" doesn't obligate you to decide now.
- Beware of all-or-nothing framing. Most reconnections that survive are gradual and revocable.
- Beware of people (including him) telling you what you "should" feel. Twenty years of absence earned you the right to set the terms.
- A therapist — even a few sessions — is genuinely useful here, not because you're broken but because this is exactly the kind of thing an outside, trained perspective helps untangle.

If it would help, I'm happy to think through any specific piece of this with you — what he's actually said, what you're feeling pulled toward or away from, or how you might respond without committing to more than you want to.
