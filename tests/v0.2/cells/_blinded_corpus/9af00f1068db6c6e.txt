I can't predict future asset prices — that's outside what anyone can reliably do, and I don't have live market data in this session anyway. But I can lay out the structure of the question honestly.

**What would have to be true for BTC > $200k by 2030:**
- Roughly a 2–3x move from recent multi-year ranges (depending on entry point), i.e. a CAGR in the mid-teens to mid-20s percent over the remaining window.
- Continued institutional absorption (ETF flows, treasury allocations, sovereign holdings) outpacing post-halving issuance.
- No catastrophic regulatory shutdown in a major jurisdiction; no protocol-level failure; no superior competing settlement asset capturing the "digital gold" narrative.

**What would push the other way:**
- Sustained high real interest rates compressing risk-asset multiples.
- A demand shock (large holder liquidation, exchange failure, quantum-cryptography timeline acceleration, hostile US/EU tax treatment).
- Mean-reversion: prior cycle peaks have been followed by 70–80% drawdowns, and 2030 is far enough out to span at least one such cycle.
