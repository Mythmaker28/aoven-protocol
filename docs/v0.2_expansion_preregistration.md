## §1 — Target n per cell

**Pre-registered.** v0.2 uses a sealed primary set of **n = 20 questions** plus a sealed hold-out of **n = 10 questions**, both filed before generation, contamination-gated identically to the v0.1.2 hold-out (`reconciliation_holdout_v0.1.2.md`).

**Cell definition.** A cell is one (rater × question × condition × layer) tuple. With 6 raters × 20 primary Q × 2 conditions (Test A, Test B) × Layer 1 = **240 Layer-1 primary ratings per dimension**, plus 6 × 20 × 1 × Layer 2 = 120 Layer-2 ratings per dimension on Test B only. Hold-out adds 6 × 10 × 2 = 120 Layer-1 ratings per dimension and 6 × 10 = 60 Layer-2 ratings per dimension. For pairwise quadratic-weighted κ between any two raters, n_paired = **40 paired observations per dimension** (20 Q × 2 conditions) on the primary set; **20 paired** on the hold-out; **60 paired** if the two sets are pooled for an audit-only κ.

**Justification for n.** The minimum n is set by the κ power calculation in §4 below. n = 40 paired primary + 20 paired hold-out exceeds the §4 minimum under the v0.1.2-observed marginal distribution. The hold-out at n = 10 questions is **not** sized to power κ on its own; it is sized to apply the v0.2 ratification criterion (mean-aggregate improvement + non-regression) on a fresh, sealed dataset, exactly as `reconciliation_holdout_v0.1.2.md` did for v0.1.2. The discipline that the *criterion* must be derived on the primary set and *confirmed* on the sealed hold-out is preserved. A second-round inconclusive verdict terminates v0.2 and forces structural changes (no third re-test on the same probes).

**v0.1.2 baseline reference (observed n, observed κ).** Primary Q1–Q10 had n_paired = 20 per dim; weighted κ on D1 = 0.938, D2 = 0.754, D5 = 0.881; D3 = 0.444 (moderate); D4 = 0.318, D6 = 0.231 (low, sparse-distribution artifact, confirmed in `reconciliation_logician_independentrater.md`). Doubling n to 40 paired primary in v0.2 is the principal lever for tightening D3/D4/D6 κ; the §4 calc gives the formal justification.

## §4 — κ power analysis with stated alpha

**Pre-registered statistical setup.**
- **Statistic:** quadratic-weighted Cohen's κ (κ_w), per `AOV_TEST_PLAN_v0.1.md` §"Inter-rater reliability". For multi-rater (panel-level) κ, Fleiss' κ generalisation under quadratic weights, computed pairwise then aggregated as the unweighted mean of pairwise κ_w (the same approach as `reconciliation_logician_independentrater.md` extended to 6 raters; 15 pairs).
- **α = 0.05** two-sided.
- **Power 1 − β = 0.80**, i.e., β = 0.20 (the directive's "or tighter" floor; can be tightened to β = 0.10 if the §1 cell budget allows).
- **Null H0:** κ_w = 0 (no agreement above chance).
- **Alternative H1:** κ_w = 0.6 (substantial agreement, the v0.2 pre-registered ratification floor).
- **Test direction:** one-sided lower; we reject H0 in favour of H1 when the lower 95 % CI of observed κ_w exceeds 0.

**Formula (cited).** Donner & Eliasziw (1992) "A goodness-of-fit approach to inference procedures for the kappa statistic," *Stat Med* 11:1511–1519, equation 3, gives the variance of κ_w under H1:

> Var(κ̂_w | κ_w = κ_1, marginal P) = (1 − κ_1)² × ψ(P, κ_1) / n

where ψ(P, κ_1) is a function of the marginal distribution P over the k ordinal categories and the assumed κ_1. For quadratic weights and a 4-category ordinal scale, Cantor (1996) "Sample-size calculations for Cohen's kappa," *Psychol Methods* 1:150–153, Table 1, tabulates ψ for representative marginals; the values used here are interpolated from that table.

**Sample size formula (one-sided, H0: κ_w = 0):**

> n_paired ≥ (z_{α} + z_{β})² × Var_H1 × n / (κ_1 − 0)²
> i.e.  n_paired ≥ (z_{α} + z_{β})² × (1 − κ_1)² × ψ(P, κ_1) / κ_1²

with z_{0.05, 1-sided} = 1.645, z_{0.20} = 0.84.

**Marginal-distribution assumption (pre-registered).** From v0.1.2 observed marginals (`reconciliation_logician_independentrater.md` §"Marginal distributions per dimension"), the average across D1–D6 is approximately P̄ ≈ {0: 0.84, 1: 0.12, 2: 0.03, 3: 0.01}. v0.2 Test A questions are, by domain construction, expected to elicit somewhat *higher* failure rates (more questions in source-discipline-vulnerable D-SCI and prescription-vulnerable D-TECH/D-PRED), so we conservatively use P̄ ≈ {0: 0.78, 1: 0.16, 2: 0.05, 3: 0.01} as the design assumption. Under this P̄, ψ(P̄, 0.6) ≈ 4.2 (Cantor 1996, Table 1, interpolated for quadratic weights).

**Resulting minimum n_paired per dimension per rater pair:**

> n_paired ≥ (1.645 + 0.84)² × (1 − 0.6)² × 4.2 / 0.6² = 6.18 × 0.16 × 4.2 / 0.36 ≈ **11.5 paired cells**

**Rounded up to the nearest sealed-set boundary: n_paired ≥ 12 paired cells per dim per rater pair.**

**v0.2 cell budget vs floor.** Primary set delivers n_paired = 40 (20 Q × 2 conditions); hold-out delivers n_paired = 20. Both exceed the 12-cell floor by a comfortable margin. The margin is intentional because Cantor (1996) tabulations assume balanced rater marginals; v0.1.2 D7/D8 showed that under highly skewed rater marginals (e.g., Logician's uniform "+1 prose cost" pattern), realised variance is higher than tabulated. With n_paired = 40, even a 2× variance underestimate still leaves power ≥ 0.80 to detect κ_w = 0.6 vs 0.

**Sensitivity check (pre-registered).** If observed v0.2 marginals turn out skewed beyond the design assumption (i.e., post-hoc ψ > 6 for any dimension), the Layer 1 κ for that dimension is reported with a wider Bonferroni-style CI and is **not** counted toward the v0.2 ratification verdict for that dimension; the dimension is flagged as "under-powered, post-hoc" and rolled forward to v0.3. This is the v0.2 analogue of the v0.1 D4/D6 sparse-distribution artifact handling.

The dimension is suspended for the v0.2 ratification arithmetic and routed to v0.3 with a rubric-clarification mandate. The protocol-level pass/fail (per §5) is computed only over κ-clearing dimensions, with the κ-failed dimensions reported transparently.

## §6 — Cross-domain leave-one-out overfit guard

**Pre-registered procedure.**

1. Compute the v0.2 ratification verdict (the §5 three-part rule) **five times**:
   - **All-domains:** 20 primary Q across {D-SCI, D-TECH, D-NORM, D-PRED}.
   - **LOO-SCI:** 14 Q across {D-TECH, D-NORM, D-PRED}; D-SCI's 6 Q removed.
   - **LOO-TECH:** 15 Q across {D-SCI, D-NORM, D-PRED}; D-TECH's 5 Q removed.
   - **LOO-NORM:** 15 Q across {D-SCI, D-TECH, D-PRED}; D-NORM's 5 Q removed.
   - **LOO-PRED:** 16 Q across {D-SCI, D-TECH, D-NORM}; D-PRED's 4 Q removed.

2. **Decision rule for v0.2 PASS.** All four LOO subsets must independently satisfy the §5 three-part rule. The all-domains verdict is reported but is not load-bearing if all four LOOs pass.

3. **Decision rule for v0.2 FAIL on overfit grounds.** If the all-domains verdict is PASS but any LOO subset returns FAIL, the v0.2 protocol is declared **domain-overfit on that domain**. The protocol is not ratified; the overfit domain is documented and v0.3 must address whether the failed-LOO outcome is (a) genuine domain-dependence of Aoven's effect, (b) a question-construction artifact within the held-out domain, or (c) a κ-power floor effect from the smaller LOO-N. Pre-registered: outcome (c) does **not** rescue v0.2 ratification — the directive's overfit guard is structural, not statistical.

4. **Decision rule for INCONCLUSIVE on LOO grounds.** If the all-domains verdict is INCONCLUSIVE, LOO is reported but does not change the overall verdict. If the all-domains verdict is PASS and 1 of 4 LOOs is INCONCLUSIVE while the other 3 are PASS, the result is reported as PASS-with-LOO-caveat and the caveat is recorded in the canonical AOVEN_PROTOCOL_v0.2.md changelog.

**Expected behavior under null and under intended effect.**

- **Under H0 (Aoven has no effect; Test A and Test B are statistically indistinguishable).** All five verdicts (all-domains and four LOOs) return FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE on the aggregate-improvement leg of the three-part rule. The cross-domain agreement of the FAIL outcome is itself the signal that null-Aoven would produce — there is no overfit to detect because there is no effect to be overfit.
- **Under H1 (Aoven has the v0.1.2-observed effect, replicating across domains).** All five verdicts return PASS, with the four LOO aggregate-improvement % values clustering tightly around the all-domains value (we pre-register: SD across the four LOO improvements ≤ 1/3 of the all-domains improvement). Tight clustering is the intended signature.
- **Under H2 (Aoven works on a subset of domains, fails on others — domain-dependent).** All-domains may return PASS by averaging, but at least one LOO returns FAIL. This is the failure mode the guard is designed to catch and is the principal reason the directive named it non-negotiable.

**Why a 4-domain set was chosen for the guard.** With 3 domains (the directive minimum), each LOO subset has 2 remaining domains; the smallest-N LOO subset would be at the κ-power margin from §4. With 4 domains, each LOO retains 3 domains and the smallest LOO subset (LOO-SCI: 14 Q × 2 cond = 28 paired cells per dim per rater pair) still exceeds the §4 floor of 12. `[Logician inference, AOV-90]` — this is the principal quantitative reason for the §2 four-domain choice.
