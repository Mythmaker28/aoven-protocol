# MAB-4 / Test B (Aoven v0.1.2 long-form)

**Prompt:** Will room-temperature ambient-pressure superconductors be discovered in the next decade?

---

[Aoven v0.1.2]

[FACT] As of late 2024, no material has been independently and reproducibly demonstrated to superconduct at room temperature and ambient pressure; high-pressure hydride claims (LK-99, hydrogen sulfide, lanthanum hydride) either failed reproduction or required extreme pressures (>100 GPa).
[FACT][CONF(medium)] The current ambient-pressure superconducting record is held by cuprate-family materials with Tc up to roughly 138 K under optimized conditions, well below room temperature.
[FACT][CONF(medium)][NOSRC] Theoretical mechanisms for room-temperature ambient-pressure superconductivity (BCS-like at 300 K, exotic pairing modes) have been proposed but lack a candidate material with quantitative theoretical support.
[INTERPRET] The 2023 LK-99 episode is informative: rapid global reproduction effort, multiple independent failures, retraction. The community is now primed for fast verification, which both lowers and raises the discovery bar (claims get tested faster, but bar for credibility is higher).
[HYP][CONF(low)] Discovery of a room-temperature ambient-pressure superconductor in the next 10 years is plausible but not the median expectation; I would assign roughly 15-30% probability based on the rate of incremental Tc improvements over the past three decades.
[HYP][CONF(medium)] Even if discovered, scaling to industrially manufacturable wire/film is a separate 10-20 year engineering problem.
[SPEC] An ML-driven materials-discovery breakthrough (e.g., from autonomous-lab or large-scale DFT screening efforts) could compress this timeline if a productive search direction is identified, but no current public evidence singles out such a direction as imminent.
[REC] Plan grid and electronics roadmaps assuming current cuprate/iron-pnictide materials remain the operational ceiling for the next 10 years; treat any room-temp claim as positive optionality rather than a baseline.
[LIMIT] I cannot evaluate post-training-cutoff materials announcements; my probability estimate would shift if a credible, independently-reproduced result has emerged since.
